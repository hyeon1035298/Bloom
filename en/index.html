Before you download.Four things to know about setup and privacy

SmartScreen warning+

Because the app isn't code-signed yet, a “Windows protected your PC” window may appear. Click More info › Run anyway.

Where it installs+

Installs to %LOCALAPPDATA%\BloomLauncher under your current account and adds a Start menu shortcut. It only installs the .NET 10 Desktop Runtime and asks for permission if you don't already have it.

What is sent to AI+

Only the questions and attachments you send, screen areas selected with Bloom Lens, text to translate, and results from PC action tools are sent.

API keys are encrypted by Windows, stored only on this PC, and used only when making requests to their respective services.

Updates and uninstall+

Checks once for a new version after launch and applies it the next time you start Bloom. You can turn off automatic checks in Settings › About.

In Settings › Data › Uninstall Bloom, you can choose whether to delete your chats and settings too. Uninstalling from Windows Settings › Apps leaves your data behind.
